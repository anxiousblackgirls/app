Depression does not always look like crying in bed. For many Black women it looks like being tired all the time, short with everyone, and numb to things that used to bring joy.

## Signs to watch for

Depression is more than a bad week. Doctors look for symptoms that last most of the day, nearly every day, for at least two weeks:

- Low mood, or feeling empty or numb
- Losing interest in things you used to enjoy
- Sleeping much more or much less than usual
- Changes in appetite or weight
- Feeling exhausted even after rest
- Irritability and a short temper
- Trouble concentrating or making decisions
- Headaches, body aches, or stomach problems with no clear cause
- Feeling worthless or guilty
- Thoughts of death or suicide

> A large national study found that Black Americans had lower lifetime rates of major depression than white Americans. When Black Americans did have depression, it tended to last longer, be more severe, and go untreated more often. Depression in our community is less likely to be noticed and more likely to be carried alone.

## Why it gets missed

Irritability and physical pain are common signs of depression that people often read as attitude or stress. If you are still getting everything done, people may assume you are fine. Functioning is not the same as being well.

## What helps

Depression responds to treatment. Therapy, medication, or both help most people feel better. Movement, sunlight, regular sleep, and time with people who love you also help, and they work best alongside care.

## Try this

- **Check the two-week mark.** Look back at the last two weeks. Count how many of the signs above you've had most days. If it's five or more, or any thoughts of death, talk to a provider this week.
- **Do one small thing.** Depression shrinks your world. Pick one tiny action today: a shower, a five-minute walk outside, or a text to a friend.
- **Say it out loud to one person.** "I think I might be depressed" is a strong first sentence.

If you are having thoughts of suicide, call or text 988 right now. You can also go to the Help tab.
