"You picked up a little weight, huh?" "Girl, you're getting too skinny." "You're not going to eat all that, are you?" At the cookout, at church or over the holidays, comments about your body can come before "hello." They often come from people who love you, and they can still sting for days.

## Comments shape how we see ourselves

Family talk about weight sticks, especially when it starts young. A study of more than 2,300 teens and their parents found that when parents talked about their kids' weight or size, the teens were more likely to diet and use unhealthy weight-control behaviors. When parents talked only about eating healthy food, without mentioning weight, the teens were less likely to diet. Many adults are still carrying comments they heard at 12.

## Why family comments hit so hard

Your aunties and grandmothers may see commenting on your body as caring, or as just how the family talks. Some of them heard the same comments growing up. That history can explain the habit without making it okay. You're allowed to protect your peace at the dinner table.

> In our families, food and bodies are always part of the conversation: "Fix her a plate," "she's got the family hips," "you need some meat on your bones." Some of it feels like love, and some of it feels like being watched. You get to decide which comments you take in and which ones you leave on the table.

## Try this

- **Have a short line ready.** "I'm not talking about my body today. How's your garden?" Then change the subject.
- **Talk to one person ahead of time.** "Auntie, I love you. Comments about my weight really hurt. Please don't bring it up."
- **Use your allies.** Ask a cousin or sibling to jump in and change the subject when the comments start.
- **Take a break.** Step outside, go help in the kitchen or leave early if you need to.
- **Debrief after.** Call a friend, journal or practice a kind phrase for yourself on the drive home.
- **Break the cycle with the kids.** Compliment what they do, not their size, and talk about food without talking about weight.
