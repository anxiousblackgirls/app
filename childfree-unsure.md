Maybe you don't feel a strong pull toward motherhood, but you're not ready to rule it out. Maybe your answer changes every few months. Not knowing yet is a real place to be, even if everyone around you seems sure.

## Uncertainty is common

In a 2024 Pew Research Center survey of adults 50 and older without children, 38 percent said there was a time they wanted kids, 32 percent never wanted them, and 25 percent weren't sure. For many, the top reason they didn't have children was simply that it didn't happen. Life, partners, health, money, and timing all shape this decision, and many people never feel completely certain. A Michigan study also found that adults without children, whether by choice or by circumstance, were about as satisfied with their lives as parents.

> Black women often face this question with a clock and an audience. Family members may treat your 30th birthday like a deadline. You may feel you have to choose between career and children because you can't count on support for both. Some women stay unsure because they haven't found a partner they trust, or because they're healing from their own childhood. These are good reasons to take your time. Feeling pressure to decide now can make it harder to hear what you actually want.

## When the answer becomes "not having kids" without choosing it

Some women end up without children because of timing, health, or a relationship that didn't work out. That can bring grief, even if you were never sure. That grief is valid, and it can sit alongside a full, happy life.

## Try this

- **Imagine both futures.** Write a short description of a day in your life at 50 with children, then without. Notice which one brings relief, sadness, or excitement.
- **Separate "want" from "should."** List every reason you're considering children, and mark which ones come from you and which come from others.
- **Talk to your doctor about your options.** If timing is a worry, ask about your fertility and options like egg freezing, so the decision feels less rushed.
- **Talk to women on both paths.** Ask mothers and childfree women what they love and what's hard.
- **Work with a therapist.** Uncertainty often has roots in fear, past hurts, or pressure. Talking it through can help you get clear.
