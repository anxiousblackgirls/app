Anxious Black Girls® is a mental health app made by licensed therapist Deseray Wilson, aka "The Anxious Black Girl." She created this app for high-achieving Black women who need a space just for them, where they can show up and receive support without having to give anything back. Here, you're seen, supported and validated in your experiences, and empowered to put your needs first.

Everything you need fits in your pocket: daily affirmations, one-minute coping skills, research-backed articles about our experiences as Black women, plans and challenges that keep you accountable, and a private journal. It's a one-stop shop for the girlies who simply want to do the work.

Meet Deseray

Deseray Wilson, LMHC · Founder

Deseray is a licensed mental health counselor and the founder of Anxious Black Girls®. Anxiety runs in her family, and watching the women before her stay stuck in places that didn't serve them shaped her life's work.

She has helped hundreds of high-achieving Black women navigate their anxiety, relationships, and life transitions. She built this app so the support she gives in session can go with you everywhere else.
